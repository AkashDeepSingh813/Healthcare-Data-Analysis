Healthcare Data Analysis 

📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive healthcare analytics dashboard to evaluate hospital network performance using 54,966 unique patient admission records spanning May 2019 to May 2024.

The dashboard explores patient demographics, medical conditions, insurance distribution, billing patterns, and emergency care demand to generate actionable business insights.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze patient demographics and medical condition distribution.

Evaluate insurance provider coverage and payer mix.

Monitor billing trends and identify high-cost patients.

Analyze emergency admissions and abnormal test results.

Identify operational patterns and areas requiring management attention.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

HTML | CSS | JavaScript | Python | Pandas | Data Analysis | Data Visualization

📊 Dashboard Features

Executive KPIs: Total admissions, billing, average billing, patient age, and emergency admissions.

Patient Analysis: Distribution across medical conditions and age groups.

Insurance Analysis: Comparison of five insurance providers.

Billing Analysis: Monthly average billing trends and high-cost patient identification.

Emergency Analysis: Monthly emergency admission trends and abnormal result monitoring.

Interactive Filtering: Dynamic charts, selectable categories, year filters, and reset functionality.

💡 Key Insights

Patient Volume: Analyzed 54,966 unique admission records.

Medical Conditions: Arthritis recorded the highest patient count with 9,218 patients.

Insurance Distribution: Cigna covered the largest number of patients (11,139), while overall payer distribution remained balanced.

Billing: Average billing per admission was approximately $25,595.

Emergency Admissions: 18,102 admissions (32.9%) were classified as emergencies.

Clinical Review: 6,038 emergency cases had abnormal test results.

High-Cost Patients: Identified 5,497 patients in the top 10% of billing amounts.

📈 Business Conclusion

The analysis highlights consistent billing patterns, balanced insurance distribution, and year-round emergency care demand. It also identifies abnormal emergency cases and high-cost patient groups requiring additional management attention.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of healthcare operations to support performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.
